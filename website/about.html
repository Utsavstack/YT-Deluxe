About YT Deluxe

YT Deluxe is a free, open-source, full-stack media management application that lets you search, preview, and download YouTube videos and audio - with a beautifully crafted Liquid Glass UI that feels premium.

Most downloaders are flooded with ads, malware, confusing interfaces, and paywalls. YT Deluxe was built to fix all of that: 100% ad-free, self-hosted, with no trackers, supporting 144p to 8K across multiple formats. Think of it as your personal YouTube client - clean, fast, and fully yours.

Future Vision

YT Deluxe is continuously evolving. Our roadmap includes playlist batch downloading, subtitle embedding, and native progress notifications.

We are also exploring cloud sync for download history, mobile PWA support, and a plugin system for custom post-processors.

What YT Deluxe Can Do

Search & Preview

Search YouTube by keyword or paste a direct URL. Browse rich video cards with thumbnails, durations, view counts, and hover previews.

High-Quality Downloads

Download from 144p up to 8K. Supports MP4, MKV, WebM, M4A, Opus, and MP3 with automatic FFmpeg merging for best quality.

Precision Trimmer

Frame-accurate start and end point selection with a live preview player. Download only the exact segment you need.

Advanced Format Control

Choose exact codec streams, container formats, and audio bitrates. Full control over what you download and how.

Hybrid Architecture

Runs seamlessly as a web app in any browser or as a fully native Windows desktop application (.exe installer).

Zero Data Collection

No tracking, no telemetry, no accounts. All history and preferences stay on your device, never on a server.

Multilingual Support

Available in English, Hindi, German, and Hinglish with full i18n support for easy community contributions.

History & Management

Track, filter, and manage all your downloads. Bulk delete, storage usage monitor, and re-download support built in.
