Search & Live Preview

Don't download the wrong video again. YT-Deluxe features a rich search experience with real-time results. Simply hover over any result to trigger a live video preview, allowing you to verify content before committing to a download.

  • Paste direct URLs or search by keywords
  • Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mini-player support
  • Rich metadata: views, duration, and channel info

8K Ultra HD & HDR

Experience media as it was intended. From mobile-friendly 144p to cinema-grade 8K resolution. YT-Deluxe automatically selects the best available streams for video and audio, merging them seamlessly with FFmpeg.

Formats MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV
Audio MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC

Zero-Loss Trimmer

Don't waste space on a 2-hour video when you only need a 30-second clip. Our precision trimmer uses FFmpeg stream copying to cut video and audio without any quality loss and with near-instant speed.

1
Drag timeline handles to select range
2
Preview the exact clip in the player
3
Download instantly with no re-encoding
