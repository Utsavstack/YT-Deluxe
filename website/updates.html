Detailed FAQ

General Questions

What makes YT Deluxe different?

Unlike other downloaders, YT Deluxe combines a premium desktop-class UI with powerful backend tools like yt-dlp. It's completely open-source, features zero ads, and respects your privacy by processing everything locally.

Is it really 100% free?

Yes. YT Deluxe is an open-source project licensed under GPL-3.0. There are no hidden costs, subscriptions, or "pro" versions. Every feature is available to everyone for free.

Installation & Updates

Which operating systems are supported?

Currently, YT Deluxe is optimized for Windows 10 and 11. We are exploring macOS and Linux builds for future releases.

How do I update to the latest version?

Simply download the latest installer from our Releases page and run it. It will automatically detect your previous installation and update the files while keeping your settings intact.

Usage & Features

Can I download entire playlists?

Yes! Simply paste the playlist URL, and YT Deluxe will analyze all videos in the playlist, allowing you to select specific ones or download the entire batch in your preferred quality.

What formats are supported?

We support all major formats including MP4, MKV, WebM for video, and MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC for audio. You can also select specific codecs like H.264, H.265 (HEVC), or AV1.

Privacy & Safety

Does YT Deluxe store my download history?

Download history is stored locally on your machine for your convenience. None of this data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party. You can clear your history anytime in the settings.

Why does my antivirus flag the installer?

As an open-source project, our installer might sometimes trigger false positives because it isn't signed with an expensive commercial certificate. You can safely bypass this or build from source.
